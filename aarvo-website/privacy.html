Privacy Policy

This policy describes personal information handled when customers browse AARVO, create an account, place orders or contact support.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, mobile number and email address, where provided.
  • Order information, product selections, payment status and invoice details.
  • Delivery details such as recipient name, address, PIN code and contact number.
  • Support messages and information voluntarily provided to resolve a request.
  • Basic technical and diagnostic information needed to secure and operate the service.

2. How information is used

We use relevant information to create and manage accounts, process orders, coordinate sellers and delivery partners, provide customer support, issue invoices or refunds, prevent fraud, maintain security and meet legal obligations.

3. Sharing

Information needed to fulfil an order may be shared with the relevant seller, payment provider, courier/delivery partner and service providers supporting the platform. We do not sell customers’ personal information. Payment credentials such as UPI PINs, card PINs or banking passwords are handled by the payment provider and must never be shared with AARVO.

4. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including accounting, dispute resolution and legal requirements. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational retention requirements. Use the AARVO in-app support channel and identify the account or order concerned. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

6. Children

AARVO marketplace accounts and purchases should be made by adults or under the supervision of a parent or lawful guardian.

7. Policy updates

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published here.
