Contact AARVO

For help with an order, payment, delivery, seller listing or privacy request, please contact AARVO through the in-app support option.

Customer support

For an existing order, open the AARVO app and use the available Help or Support option. Please keep your order ID ready so the support team can locate the request.

What to include

  • Order ID, if your question is about an order.
  • Registered mobile number (share it only through the private support channel).
  • A short description of the issue and relevant photos, if applicable.

Business location

AARVO operates its marketplace services in India. Service availability depends on the delivery PIN code and seller/courier coverage.

Business contact details: AARVO’s verified support email, customer-care phone number and registered business address must be added here before payment-gateway review. We have not published unverified contact information.

Response

Support requests are reviewed during business operating hours. For urgent payment issues, do not share OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or banking passwords with anyone claiming to represent AARVO.
