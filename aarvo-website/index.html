Customer information & policies

Please review these policies before placing an order. The policies apply to orders placed through AARVO when the relevant service is live.

Privacy Policy

AARVO may collect information needed to provide marketplace services, such as contact details, delivery information, order details and support requests. We use this information to operate the service, process orders, coordinate delivery, prevent misuse and provide customer support.

We share only relevant information with service providers such as payment and delivery partners when needed to complete a transaction. We do not display sensitive account or payment credentials publicly. Payment details are handled by the relevant payment provider; AARVO does not ask customers to share OTPs or passwords with support staff.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact AARVO using the support channel shown in the app or the official contact details published on this website.

Terms of Use

By using AARVO, customers agree to provide accurate account, contact and delivery information and to use the platform lawfully. Product descriptions, prices, stock and delivery estimates are provided by participating sellers and may change before an order is confirmed.

An order is subject to seller acceptance, stock availability, successful payment where applicable and serviceability of the delivery address. AARVO may cancel or decline an order where necessary to prevent fraud, comply with law or resolve an operational issue.

Cancellation, Returns & Refunds

Cancellation and return eligibility may depend on the product type, seller terms and applicable consumer law. Customers should raise a request through the AARVO support channel as soon as possible. Perishable, hygiene-sensitive, custom or used products may have restrictions where permitted by law.

Where a refund is approved, it will be initiated to the original payment method or another lawful method agreed with the customer. The time taken for the amount to appear depends on the payment provider and bank. Any applicable return, replacement or refund decision will be communicated to the customer.

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery availability, charges and estimated timelines are shown during checkout where supported. Delivery is subject to pickup availability, seller handover, courier serviceability, weather, local conditions and other operational factors.

Customers should provide a complete address and reachable contact details. Tracking or delivery updates will be shared when available. If delivery is delayed or unsuccessful, please contact AARVO support through the app.

Need help?

For order, seller, privacy or payment questions, please use the support option inside the AARVO app. Official business contact details will be published here once confirmed.
