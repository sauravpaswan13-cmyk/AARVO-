Shipping & Delivery

AARVO coordinates order fulfilment with participating sellers and available delivery partners. Delivery options depend on the destination and product.

1. Serviceability

Delivery is available only to PIN codes supported by the relevant seller and delivery partner. Customers can confirm serviceability during checkout where the feature is available.

2. Dispatch and delivery estimates

Estimated dispatch and delivery information, where available, is shown during checkout or in order updates. Estimates are indicative and may change due to seller preparation time, courier capacity, weather, public holidays, local restrictions or other operational conditions.

3. Delivery charges

Any applicable delivery charge is displayed before payment confirmation. Charges may vary by destination, parcel size or weight, product type and courier service.

4. Tracking and delivery

Tracking information is shared when it is made available by the delivery partner. Customers should ensure the delivery address and contact number are correct and that someone is available to receive the parcel.

5. Delayed, failed or returned deliveries

If a parcel is delayed, marked delivered but not received, or returned to the seller, contact AARVO support through the app with the order ID. The team will review available seller and courier records and communicate the next steps.

6. Delivery issues

For damaged, incorrect or missing items, please refer to the Cancellation, Returns & Refunds Policy and raise a request promptly after delivery.
