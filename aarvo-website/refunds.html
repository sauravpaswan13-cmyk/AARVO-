Cancellation, Returns & Refunds

AARVO aims to make cancellation and refund requests clear. Eligibility depends on product condition, order status, seller terms and applicable consumer law.

1. Order cancellation

Customers may request cancellation as soon as possible through the AARVO app support option. Cancellation is generally possible before the seller has dispatched or handed the order to the courier. Once dispatched, the request may be handled as a return or delivery refusal where permitted.

2. Wrong, damaged or missing items

If an order arrives damaged, defective, incorrect or incomplete, contact AARVO support promptly, preferably within 48 hours of delivery. Share the order ID and clear photographs of the product and outer packaging. Requests will be reviewed with the seller and delivery partner.

3. Product-specific restrictions

Fresh, perishable, consumable, hygiene-sensitive, customised or used products may not be eligible for change-of-mind returns where permitted by law. This does not affect rights for defective, damaged, incorrect or otherwise legally returnable goods.

4. Refund approval and timing

When a refund is approved, AARVO will initiate it to the original payment method, unless another method is lawfully agreed with the customer. AARVO aims to initiate approved refunds within 5–7 business days after approval. The time for the amount to appear in the customer’s account depends on the bank or payment provider.

5. Failed or duplicate payments

If money is debited but an order is not confirmed, do not repeatedly retry payment. Contact support with the transaction reference and relevant order details. After payment-provider reconciliation, an eligible refund will be initiated.

6. How to request help

Open the AARVO app and use the in-app support option. Include the order ID, issue details and supporting photographs where relevant. Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or banking password.

Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies available to consumers under applicable Indian law.
