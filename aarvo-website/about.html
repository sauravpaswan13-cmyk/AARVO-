About AARVO

AARVO is an online marketplace designed to make discovery and shopping for everyday essentials, local products and seasonal or festival needs convenient.

What AARVO does

AARVO provides a digital marketplace where participating sellers can list products and customers can discover available items, compare displayed prices and place orders when the relevant checkout service is enabled.

Our marketplace approach

  • Product listings and stock information are supplied by participating sellers.
  • Customers can review product information and applicable charges before placing an order.
  • Order fulfilment and delivery depend on seller acceptance, stock and serviceability.
  • Customer support is available through the support option in the AARVO app.

Our focus

We aim to provide a clear, reliable and convenient shopping experience while helping local sellers reach customers online. AARVO services and delivery coverage may be introduced or expanded in stages.

AARVO is a marketplace platform. Product availability, fulfilment and applicable seller-specific conditions are shown with the relevant listing or at checkout.
