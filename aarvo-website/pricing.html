Pricing & Charges

AARVO displays product prices in Indian Rupees (INR). The final payable amount is shown before a customer confirms an order.

Product prices

Each live product listing displays its seller-provided price in INR. Prices and availability may change; the price shown in the checkout summary before order confirmation is the applicable displayed price for that order.

Taxes

Where applicable, taxes are included in or separately identified against the product price or invoice in accordance with the seller’s tax status and applicable law. Any tax details shown at checkout or on the invoice form part of the order total.

Delivery charges

Delivery charges, if applicable, depend on the delivery PIN code, order contents, seller and available courier service. The applicable charge is shown before payment confirmation. If delivery is unavailable for an address, the order cannot be completed for that address.

Other charges

AARVO will disclose any applicable platform or service charge before the customer confirms payment. No undisclosed charge should be added after order confirmation.

AARVO does not publish a single fixed delivery fee for every order. The amount, where applicable, is calculated and displayed for the specific order and destination.

Price questions

For a price or invoice query, contact AARVO through the in-app support option and mention the product or order ID.
