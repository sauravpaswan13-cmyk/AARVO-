# AARVO Legal Policy Templates

These are starting templates, not legal advice. A business/legal owner should adapt and approve them for the jurisdictions and products served by AARVO before publication.

## Terms of Service

AARVO provides a marketplace platform connecting buyers and independent sellers. Buyers agree to provide accurate account, delivery and payment information. Sellers are responsible for the accuracy, legality, availability, quality and fulfilment of their listings. AARVO may suspend accounts or listings for fraud, abuse, policy violations or legal requirements.

Orders become confirmed only after the marketplace's server verifies the applicable payment. Product availability, pricing, taxes/fees and delivery estimates are subject to the information presented at checkout and the applicable seller/provider terms.

## Privacy Policy

AARVO should disclose what personal data is collected, why it is needed, how long it is retained, which service providers receive it, how payment information is handled, and how customers can exercise applicable privacy rights. Payment credentials should be handled by the payment provider rather than stored as raw card data by AARVO.

## Cancellation / Return / Refund Policy

State clearly when an order can be cancelled, which products are returnable, applicable return windows and condition requirements, who pays return shipping, how refunds are initiated, and expected processing times. Refund decisions must be recorded against the order and payment event and should not be represented as complete until the payment provider confirms the refund where applicable.

## Seller Terms

Sellers must provide accurate product information, lawful inventory, correct stock quantities, fulfil orders within the agreed service levels, cooperate with disputes and returns, and maintain any verification/payout requirements imposed by the marketplace or payment provider. Seller fees and payout timing should be disclosed before onboarding.

## Contact / Grievance Information

Publish a monitored support email/phone and a business address where legally required. Define escalation ownership for payment issues, delivery failures, returns, seller disputes, fraud reports and privacy requests.

## Publication gate

Do not publish these templates unchanged. Replace placeholders with AARVO's actual business identity, jurisdiction, contact details, service providers, retention periods and operational commitments, then obtain appropriate legal review.
